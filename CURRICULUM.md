Version: 1.0

Author: TG + ChatGPT
Duration: 120 Days
Daily Time:
- Weekdays: 2 Hours
- Saturday: 6 Hours
- Sunday: 6 Hours

---

# Purpose

This curriculum is immutable.

It defines exactly what is taught on each day.

The Registry tracks progress.

Daily lessons are generated from this curriculum.

---

# Learning Principles

Every topic must answer:

1. How does it work?
2. Why was it designed this way?
3. What tradeoffs exist?
4. Where is it used in production?
5. How is it asked in interviews?
6. When should it NOT be used?
